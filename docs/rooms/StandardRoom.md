## 📸 Visual Highlights

The room features a warm, wood-paneled aesthetic that creates a cozy, "at-home" atmosphere. The walls are finished in a light pine wood with darker accents around the TV and workspace. The bed features premium white linens with a signature Green, Blue, and Yellow stripe, adding a splash of vibrant color to the natural wood tones.

Every room is equipped with:
